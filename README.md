# Mech-Spec-Technologies

ITExperience MID-YEAR PROJECT CHALLENGE

Brief Description of the Organization
Mech Spec Technologies is a technology company committed to developing secure, scalable, and innovative digital solutions that solve real-world challenges. As online education continues to grow, there is an increasing demand for platforms that enable instructors to monetize their expertise while providing students with accessible, engaging, and secure learning experiences.
Through this challenge, participants will design and develop the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) of a modern Learning Management Platform (LMS). The MVP should demonstrate strong software engineering principles, secure system design and an architecture capable of evolving into a large-scale educational platform.
